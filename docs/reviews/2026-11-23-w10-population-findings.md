The four imposed two-centroid partitions use all30 stored own-channel PC dimensions and deterministic initialization. They **force two groups** even if a population is continuous; the displayed separation is not a test proving two neurons. Group IDs have no biological meaning. They are saved only in this review packet and never written to `spike_clusters.npy` or a curated spike table. The cluster31 grouping instead uses cross-run time correspondence to probe the selected-run events; it does not combine outputs from two sorts.

For each pair, five nearest neighbors are found in all shared stored PC dimensions, excluding the event itself. Cluster sizes are unequal and the features were produced by the sorter being assessed. These descriptive fractions are neither formal isolation metrics nor independent validation, and no cutoff is adopted. Raw pair cohorts use12 channels nearest the first cluster's peak; this differs from W8/W9's pair-mean channel selection. Saved arrays retain the full selected spatial context.

All subgroup mean comparisons use the same eight channels and samples−20 through+40. Checking integer shifts from−3 through+3 samples leaves the highest correlation at zero for all five probes. This rules out a simple integer offset in this limited test, not fractional alignment effects, preprocessing effects, waveform states or overlapping neurons. No automatic correction is applied.
