## Interpretation and validation

All 27 new figures and eight reused pair figures were visually reviewed. Neighbor screening covers all 57 other clusters for every target; selected pair evidence uses at most two largest absolute template cosines (at least .05) plus the closest template peak within150 µm, among competitors with at least20 events. These are review prioritization rules, not validated scientific acceptance thresholds. Opposite-polarity templates are included. Sparse neighbors remain in the screening table rather than being silently accepted as absent. Mean-waveform correlations, two-dimensional feature overlap/separation and uncorrected correlograms are descriptive; none independently proves neuron identity, contamination, or a merge/split.

The independent audit reconstructed **14,368 raw excerpts / 27,815,988 values**, reproduced all 60 quarter means/correlations, checked all 15 ACGs and 12 new cross-correlograms against direct pairwise differences, independently inverted the physical PC-channel mapping and reproduced the projections, and recalculated event counts, short ISIs and guard/full-population coincidence interval membership. Every check passed. Six existing round2/W7B helper tests also passed. Raw binary SHA-256 and W7B/W7-review/W8 manifests remained unchanged. The full source recording and historical worker were not modified.

Quarter1–4 are consecutive 75 nominal seconds (750,000 samples), not four experiments. Correlations use eight nearby channels over samples −20 through +40 and a pooled reference that may share events with quarters. They are not independent statistical tests. Raw waveforms use complete ±100-sample windows, numerical scaling ×1e6, and a median baseline over −100 through −31. That scaling is not a verified microvolt calibration. The figures show only two of eight unit channels and four of twelve pair channels; saved arrays retain all selected channels.

Warnings remain annotations from the validated **2048-channel extra_4 population**, projected by sample index. They are not recomputed on this shank. Peak-channel guard membership is an event-center flag, not whole-unit unusable time; unavailable coincidence assessment is preserved in the metrics. No exposure or firing-rate correction has been adopted.
