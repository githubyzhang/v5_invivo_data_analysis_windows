### Expansion strategy: independent sorting per shank

The default plan is **shank-by-shank Kilosort sorting**, then a combined extra_4 report. This follows the shank0 workflow already being validated and gives explicit per-shank channel selection, preprocessing/drift estimates and quality review. It is a project strategy, not a claim that joint multi-shank sorting is impossible or always inferior.

Storage and sorting scope are different decisions. Separate verified 512-channel exports are the current pilot pattern; alternatively a verified full-width binary can be used with the correct channel/shank map. The installed Kilosort 4.1.7 API supports `shank_idx`; a list sorts the specified shanks sequentially into separate results. The values must match `probe['kcoords']`, not an assumed zero-based numbering. Reading a 2048-channel binary with explicit single-shank selection still means independent shank sorting, not a joint 2048-channel sort. See the [official API](https://kilosort.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api.html) and installed `kilosort/run_kilosort.py`. W13/W14 will choose and validate the input implementation; no shared-width export or joint sorter has been implemented yet.

Continue computing/using the Mac coincidence annotations at their validated full-2048-channel extra_4 population and project intervals by sample time onto each shank. Do not silently recompute those criteria using only 512 channels. In the final combined result use identities including recording, shank, selected run and cluster/curation revision. Combining distinct shanks is different from mixing alternative sorts of the same shank. Do not claim cross-shank neuron uniqueness without appropriate evidence; retain this as a limitation or review it if geometry/evidence warrants.

### Channel-quality criterion for expansion

Here, a channel "equivalent to 248" means a **similar quality problem**, such as persistent clipping/flatness with a large fraction of time potentially unusable. It does not mean the same local binary index or geometric location on another shank. Such channels deserve explicit assessment and may warrant exclusion or a matched sensitivity test.

However, flat-guard coverage (at least 10 identical samples plus 500-sample padding) is not itself proven clipping duration or validated sorted-unit unusable time. Check raw values, duration/persistence, temporal clustering, neighboring signal support and source/acquisition context. Distinguish a persistently compromised channel from brief shared episodes affecting many otherwise usable channels. No universal percentage threshold or automatic transfer of the b248 exclusion is adopted; W11/W13 must specify any defensible criterion before broad application.

By W13 we should have a repeatable procedure and know what evidence to collect for the remaining shanks. We will not yet know their individual bad channels, cluster identities, unit yields or necessary exceptions. The same workflow stages transfer; identical decisions and parameter adequacy are not guaranteed. Extra_5, extra_6 and stimulation-specific handling remain outside this extra_4 milestone. SpikeInterface remains deferred.
